Education & Community, India

One School. One Decade. A Different Tomorrow.

Doddaballapura Government School

We didn’t want to simply help a school perform better.

We wanted to help its students imagine a better life.

Our long term association with a government school in Doddaballapura has extended beyond infrastructure: school buses, security, meals, a dedicated computer laboratory and ongoing support for student development. The school has maintained a 100% pass percentage for the last decade.

But the real story begins after the examination.

Students who score above 60% in SSLC are given opportunities for on the job training and employment with nearby factories and companies.

Education should not simply help a child pass an exam. It should help them see what life could look like after it.
