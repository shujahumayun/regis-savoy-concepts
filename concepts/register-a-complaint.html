SEBI SCORES

For lodging grievances online with SEBI, please visit scores.sebi.gov.in and register on the SCORES portal.

The following details are mandatory at the time of registration:

  • Name
  • PAN
  • Address
  • Mobile Number
  • Email ID

Registering on SCORES ensures effective communication and speedy redressal of your grievance.
