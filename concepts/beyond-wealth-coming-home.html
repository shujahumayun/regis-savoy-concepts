A Story About Being There When It Matters

She Refused to Board.

The Three Dogs Who Came Home

Then came a moment that made this belief deeply personal.

At the height of the COVID‑19 pandemic,

a young girl was stranded in New York,

thousands of miles from her family in India.

The world had stopped.

Borders were closing.

Flights were disappearing.

International travel had become extraordinarily difficult.

But there was one thing she would not leave behind:

her three dogs.

To the world, they were animals.

To her, they were family.

And leaving family behind was never going to be an option.

Getting one person home during the pandemic was difficult enough.

Bringing three pets across continents, through unprecedented restrictions and regulations, seemed almost impossible.

So we stopped looking for the usual route.

We reached out across the system,

working through complex regulations, logistics and cross‑border requirements,

and taking the matter directly to regional and national policymakers.

There were days of uncertainty.

There were closed doors.

But we kept going.

Eventually, the permissions came through.

The paperwork was cleared.

The tickets were secured.

And after weeks of anxiety and separation,

a granddaughter and her three companions boarded a flight home to India.

When they finally stepped into the arms of a waiting family,

something much bigger than a journey had been completed.

A family was whole again.

And that is perhaps the simplest expression of what we believe:

We look beyond portfolios to the lives those portfolios exist to support. And when it really matters, we believe in being there.
