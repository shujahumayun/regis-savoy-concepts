Impact Sector 02

Education & Opportunity

We did not want to simply help a school perform better. We wanted to help its students imagine a better life.

Our work in education goes beyond buildings, to the moment a student walks out of the examination hall and into a future.

  • 800+

    students at one school today, from 15 a decade ago

  • 100%

    pass rate, maintained throughout

  • 15%

    IRR returned by one Development Impact Bond

  • Doddaballapura, India

    One school. One decade. A different tomorrow.

    School buses, security, meals and a computer laboratory, as one school grew from 15 students to over 800.

    Read the story

  • After the Exam

    Life after the examination.

    Students who score above 60% in SSLC are offered on the job training and employment with nearby companies.

  • Narok, Kenya

    Girls who stay in school.

    Helping girls continue their education at Nkoilale Primary School, in a remote part of Narok.

  • Development Impact Bonds

    Paying for outcomes, not activity.

    Working with policymakers to advance Development Impact Bonds in India. One such bond returned an IRR of 15%.

  • Everyday Care

    The everyday things that matter.

    Meals, deworming, and health and hygiene programmes, alongside the classroom.

  • Rural India

    More than 2,500 rural students.

    Reached across around 400 schools, through 17 completed education and community projects.
